# Prescient Healthcare Group

- **URL:** https://www.prescienthg.com/
- **Captured:** 2026-10-05 (`shots/prescient-desktop.jpg`, `shots/prescient-mobile.jpg`). A bot check blanked one section mid-page; the rest rendered.
- **Maps to:** landing

## Worth borrowing
- Hero structure: one-line positioning ("The strategy-to-impact partner for life sciences companies"), a two-sentence paragraph that names the practices, then two CTAs side by side: a secondary "Explore intelligence" and a primary "Contact Us".
- A proof strip immediately under the hero: "60+ pharma and biotech clients", "5,000+ projects delivered globally". Numbers before any marketing copy.
- Four practice cards on a pale lilac field, each with a two-line description and one link. Clear, scannable, equal weight.

## Not for us
- Dark photo hero with reduced-contrast text; the headline sits over a blurred lab image and reads soft.
- The full-screen cookie preference centre.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- Hero + proof strip + practice cards is the layout Tractum's landing page is already heading toward. The dual CTA (explore vs contact) maps to the dual contact requirement.
