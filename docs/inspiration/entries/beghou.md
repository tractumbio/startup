## Worth borrowing
- Three cards led by a verb with the verb emphasised: "**Build** strong, flexible technology and data foundations", "**Enable** agile and aligned execution", "**Engage** providers, payers and patients". Verb-first cards suit Tractum's pipeline and automation offer.
- A single primary CTA repeated ("Contact our experts"), never two competing buttons.
- The circular services diagram with the client's stakeholders (Providers / Payers / Patients) at the centre: a way to show that the services serve one goal.

## Not for us
- Generative line-art illustrations are pretty but mean nothing; Tractum's inline SVG diagrams should show the actual mechanism.
- Promotional ribbon above the nav and a cookie modal over the hero: two interruptions before the content.
- Pink accent on navy is a strong brand choice that would read as borrowed.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- Steal the verb-led card copy pattern for the CRO page's three capabilities.
