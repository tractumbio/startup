# ClearView Healthcare Partners

- **URL:** https://www.clearviewhcp.com/ (not clearviewhc.com, which is an unrelated real-estate investor)
- **Captured:** 2026-10-05 (`shots/clearview-desktop.jpg`, `shots/clearview-mobile.jpg`)
- **Maps to:** landing, biotech-consulting

## Worth borrowing
- Full-bleed aerial forest photo with a short centred claim ("Gain confidence in the path forward.") and one subline. Calm, confident, no jargon.
- Practice-area selector directly under the hero: a vertical list on the left (Pharma & Biotech, MedTech, Pricing & Market Access, Analytics, Launch) with a description and one link on the right. A good pattern for Tractum's two doors if they ever become three or four.
- Restrained green palette, white space, one "Learn more" button per block. Nothing competes.

## Not for us
- Two blocks on the home page rendered as empty white cards (lazy-loaded images that never arrived). Any image-led block needs a text fallback.
- Generic stock imagery (rowing crew for "The ClearView Way").

## Notes for the Tractum design
- The closest of the set to Tractum's current tone. Use it as the benchmark for "quiet and credible".
