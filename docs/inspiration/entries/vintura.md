# Vintura

- **URL:** https://www.vintura.com/
- **Captured:** 2026-10-05 (`shots/vintura-desktop.jpg`, `shots/vintura-mobile.jpg`). The desktop page overflowed to 3654px wide in the capture, so the layout has a horizontal-scroll bug.
- **Maps to:** landing, contact

## Worth borrowing
- Serif display headline on a dark teal field, with one word underlined in orange ("Creating meaningful impact in healthcare & life sciences"). The one accent carries the whole hero.
- Named-person CTAs instead of generic buttons: "Natalia is happy to tell you more", "Silvia will tell you more". For a founder-led practice this is the right register: the person is the product.
- Small-caps, letter-spaced nav and labels; a single pill button; editorial white sections with a section label ("\ LIFE SCIENCES") above each heading.

## Not for us
- Horizontal overflow on desktop; broken image placeholder in the hero tile row.
- Two-audience split (Life Sciences / Healthcare) is done in the nav only, not on the landing page, so a first-time visitor has to guess.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- Strongest typographic reference in the set. Serif headline + dark teal + one warm accent is a palette worth testing against Tractum's blue mark.
- Adopt the named CTA for the contact blocks: "Adrian will reply within a day" rather than "Contact us".
