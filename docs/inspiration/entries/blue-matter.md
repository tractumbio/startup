# Blue Matter

- **URL:** https://www.bluematterconsulting.com/
- **Captured:** 2026-10-05 (`shots/blue-matter-desktop.jpg`, `shots/blue-matter-mobile.jpg`)
- **Maps to:** cro-services, biotech-consulting

## Worth borrowing
- One plain sentence under the headline that says what the firm is and for whom ("a strategic consulting firm serving the life sciences industry"). Tractum's hero should pass the same test.
- "Strategic Advisory" section split into two columns, Cross-Functional Strategy and Functional Expertise, each a list of named service tiles. The two-column split is a clean way to show breadth without a wall of prose.
- Featured publication block (report cover, title, one line, link) as the first thing after the hero: proof of thinking, not a claim.

## Not for us
- Headline "New Ideas. Better Results." could be any firm in any industry.
- Fourteen gradient tiles in a row read as a menu, not a story; Tractum has fewer services and should name them in sentences.
- Purple glow background art.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- Use the two-column service list idea on cro-services (for example "What we build" vs "What we run").
