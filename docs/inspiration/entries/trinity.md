# Trinity Life Sciences

- **URL:** https://trinitylifesciences.com/
- **Captured:** 2026-10-05 (`shots/trinity-desktop.jpg`, `shots/trinity-mobile.jpg`). The mega-menu rendered open and broke the layout in the capture; the hero and body are readable.
- **Maps to:** landing

## Worth borrowing
- Three doors directly under the hero, each with an icon, a name and a one-line promise (Pipeline & Pre-Commercial / Launch / Brand Optimization), each ending in "Learn more". This is the same three-door device as Tractum's landing page.
- A client quote as a full-width band with the client's company named underneath.

## Not for us
- "Schedule a Demo" as the primary CTA: product language for a services firm.
- 3D glowing spheres and a nested mega-menu of forty items. Enterprise scale, not boutique.
- Headline "Powering the future of life sciences commercialization" is a slogan, not a claim.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- Confirms the three-door pattern works, and shows the failure mode when every door hides a product catalogue. Keep Tractum's doors to two or three with real first steps behind them.
