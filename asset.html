How the engagement runs

Three tracks, with a decision after each. Stopping early is a valid outcome, and usually the cheapest one. I use software to search the literature faster, but the recommendation is mine.

Track 1 · Assess

Is the eye real?

  • Mechanism → indication mapping
  • Literature + in-silico assessment
  • Ocular delivery and exposure questions
  • Preliminary patent landscape

You get indication shortlist, evidence matrix, risk register, recommendation memo.

Track 2 · Design evidence

Prove it

  • Safety / tox / efficacy study plan
  • Endpoints and acceptance criteria
  • CRO brief, selection and oversight

Who does what I design and oversee; studies run at CROs you contract.

Track 3 · Position

Defend it

  • IP strategy with your patent attorney
  • Valuation model with an assumptions register
  • Investor / partner materials

Who does what formal freedom-to-operate opinions and filings come from a qualified patent attorney.

After every track

A decision you can defend

Pauseuntil one specific piece of evidence is in
Stopwith the reasons on paper, before serious capital is spent

Why the eye, and why me

Why the eye

71.6% vs 52.0%of Phase I programs, where safety and tolerability are tested, advanced to Phase II: ophthalmology vs all indications, 2011 to 2020. The highest rate of any disease area (BIO/Informa/QLS).
55%of new drugsof novel active substances launched in the past decade were originated by emerging biopharma, not large pharma. The ones that reached patients were usually commercialised by a bigger company (IQVIA Institute).
US$66Bby 2030projected global ophthalmic drug market, up from an estimated US$37.5B in 2025, with investment concentrated in sight-threatening disease such as AMD and diabetic retinopathy (Mordor Intelligence; Cheng et al., npj Digital Medicine, 2025).
60%less timecut from early-stage development where AI is integrated, alongside a higher likelihood of clinical success, by predicting efficacy, safety and manufacturability more accurately (Cheng et al., npj Digital Medicine, 2025).
US$1.3BupfrontMerck’s 2024 EyeBio deal, plus up to US$1.7B in milestones. Buying in is how large pharma most often builds an eye pipeline.
