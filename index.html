Managing Director & Founder

Dr. Adrian Cioanca

Retinal scientist, biotech co-founder and strategy consultant. I help companies frame the development question early, and build the evidence in the order that answers it.

I spent my research years on retinal degeneration: why photoreceptors die, and what might stop them. From inside the lab I saw good science stall for reasons outside the experiment. The development question was framed late, the evidence was built in the wrong order, or the results never reached a board in terms it could act on.

The longer story

So I kept moving toward that gap. I founded Vision ACTion to put researchers, clinicians and patients in the same room, because each of them knows something the other two are guessing at. I co-founded a company to take a retinal therapeutic forward myself, and learned what it costs to be wrong late. That is the work I do now: asking the hard questions early, while they are still cheap to answer.
