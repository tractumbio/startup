## Worth borrowing
- Hero copy stack: small kicker ("Biotech & Pharma Consulting"), a two-clause headline ("Strategic Guidance. Hands-On Execution.") with a short accent underline, one paragraph, then three pill links to the service areas.
- Three-column proof row inside the hero band (Real-World Experience / Integrated Network / Flexible Support), each a heading plus two sentences. This is "why us" done before the fold.
- Sticky left sub-navigation on the long body page (Overview, Our Network, Product Development...). Useful for Tractum's about page once prior work and accolades make it long.
- Big-number proof further down: "350+" specialists, "20+" years.

## Not for us
- The solid yellow body background. Loud, and it fights the dark hero.
- Painterly dark hero image; decorative rather than specific.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- Alacrita's positioning ("built on real-world experience", practitioners who have done the work) is the same claim Tractum makes about the founder. Study how they phrase it without boasting.
