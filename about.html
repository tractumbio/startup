Why I do this work

I spent my research years on retinal degeneration: why photoreceptors die, and what might stop them. From inside the lab I saw good science stall for reasons outside the experiment. The development question was framed late, the evidence was built in the wrong order, or the results never reached a board in terms it could act on.

So I kept moving toward that gap. I founded Vision ACTion to put researchers, clinicians and patients in the same room, because each of them knows something the other two are guessing at. I co-founded a company to take a retinal therapeutic forward myself, and learned what it costs to be wrong late.

That is the work I do now: asking the hard questions early, while they are still cheap to answer, and helping CROs build the services that let them answer those questions for their clients.
