## What the firm is

Tractum Bio Consulting — an **ophthalmology-focused** scientific consulting firm.
Founded 2025. Managing Director and Founder: Dr. Adrian Cioanca.
Domain: tractumbio.com · Contact: adrian@tractumbio.com

One domain (the eye), two buyer tiers:

**Tier 1 — Small biotech → advisory & guidance.**
They lack ophthalmic expertise, infrastructure, and capability. Tractum guides them from
"is the eye a real opportunity for this asset?" through to a defensible IP and valuation
position: mechanism deep dive → indication screening → safety/efficacy plan → IP & path
to Phase 1 → valuation modelling (rNPV/DCF) → CRO matching.
Deliverable = decisions + roadmap + a valuation story the client can defend to a board.

**Tier 2 — Pharma / preclinical CROs → ophthalmic capability build.**
They already have data and research infrastructure; they want ophthalmic testing
capability in-house. Tractum layers domain capability **on top of their infrastructure**
— bespoke disease modelling, molecular/histology, imaging, transcriptomics, and the
advanced analytics layer. Deliverable = a working capability with method and know-how
transferred.

**Boundary:** capability build = enablement and method/know-how transfer. Tractum does
**not** operate an ongoing lab or CRO, and does not run the bench itself.

## Engine components (support the two tiers — never sold as standalone pillars)

- **Agentic literature intelligence.** NLP/agentic pipelines continuously monitor
  published ophthalmic research to surface emerging targets, mechanisms, and repurposing
  candidates. AI mines, ranks and surfaces; the scientist decides. Scoped strictly to
  ophthalmology.
- **Cross-study predictive insight layer.** ROADMAP, not a current deliverable. Where a
  long-running Tier-2 engagement is heading as standardized structured data accumulates.
