Managing Director & Founder

Dr. Adrian Cioanca

Retinal scientist, biotech co-founder and strategy consultant. I help companies work out the right question to ask early, then build the evidence that answers it.

I spent years in research, generating the ideas and the lead candidates, then testing them. What I came to see is that without commercialisation the science never reaches a patient. Through Vision ACTion I have also sat with people living with vision loss, and seen what changes when researchers, clinicians and drug developers work as one. So the work now is to help biotech entrepreneurs move into the eye, and to help the services that test for them exist. That is where I can make a difference.
