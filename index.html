Managing Director & Founder

Dr. Adrian Cioanca

A retinal scientist who has also been the biotech founder, the translational developer and the strategy consultant — every vantage point an assessment is built on.

I spent my research years on retinal degeneration — why photoreceptors die, and what might stop them. What became obvious from inside the lab is that the science is rarely the bottleneck. Good work stalls because the question was framed late, or the evidence was built in the wrong order, or nobody translated it into terms a board could act on.

So I kept moving toward that gap. I founded Vision ACTion to put researchers, clinicians and patients in the same room, because each of them knows something the other two are guessing at. I co-founded a company to take a retinal therapeutic forward myself, and learned what it costs to be wrong late. I now do for other companies what I wish someone had done for us: ask the hard question early, while it is still cheap to answer.
