Why me

Pharmaceutical demand is real and recent

$1.3Bupfront · up to $3B total

In 2024, Merck acquired the ophthalmology company EyeBio for $1.3 billion upfront and up to $3 billion including milestones — for a clinical-stage candidate targeting diabetic macular edema and wet AMD. It is one of several recent moves by large pharmaceutical companies to acquire and license validated ophthalmic assets rather than build the biology in-house.
