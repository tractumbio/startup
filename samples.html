What is in the pack

01

A full study report

One navigable document covering a complete worked study, in the form a sponsor receives it, not an extract.

02

The methods, laid open

How each value was computed, and how to follow any number in the report back to the data it came from.

03

Statistics, and why those ones

The models applied, the study design they were chosen for, and the reasoning behind the choice. The part most reports leave unsaid.

04

Figures as delivered

Publication-grade figures at the quality we actually deliver, so you can hold them against what you receive today.

How a go or no-go recommendation, its evidence and its stated limits are set out for a board.

06

What it replaced

A short note on the manual work the pipeline took over, and where the time used to go.

Every sample is fully anonymised. Client names, compounds, targets, identifiers and any commercially sensitive result are removed or replaced, and nothing is shared without the originating client's agreement. The samples show structure, analytical rigour and presentation, not anyone's data.
