# BioBoston Consulting

- **URL:** https://biobostonconsulting.com/
- **Captured:** 2026-10-05 (`shots/bioboston-desktop.jpg`, `shots/bioboston-mobile.jpg`). Note on the name: the request said "Bostonbio"; bostonbio.com is a parked domain for sale, and Boston Biomedical Consultants was absorbed into IMS Health (now IQVIA) in 2015. BioBoston Consulting is the active boutique with the closest name; confirm it is the firm meant.
- **Maps to:** about, cro-services

## Worth borrowing
- Four proof tiles inside the hero: "1,000+ projects delivered", "97% repeat clients", "25+ years", "650+ senior experts, including former FDA investigators". The last one is the model: a number plus the specific credential that makes it credible.
- Scrolling ticker of service names (IND, Audits, Computer System Validation...). Dense but scannable; a lightweight way to list many services without a grid.
- Named testimonials with title and employer.

## Not for us
- All-caps headline over a stock lab photo, yellow and black palette, three "Contact Us" buttons on the first screen. Reads as a vendor, not an adviser.
- Large empty bands where lazy sections failed to load.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- The proof-tile idea is right for the about page, but Tractum's numbers are smaller and should be specific rather than large ("Phase I success", named programmes) as the current site already does.
