# G&Co.

- **URL:** https://www.g-co.agency/
- **Captured:** 2026-10-05 (`shots/g-co-desktop.jpg`, `shots/g-co-mobile.jpg`)
- **Maps to:** landing (typography only)

## Worth borrowing
- Type pairing: an enormous grotesk line ("HUMAN JUDGMENT") answered by an equally large serif line ("AT THE AI FRONTIER"). The contrast between the two faces does the design work. Tractum's two audiences could be expressed with a similar pairing at a sane size.
- Confident use of black bands between white sections.

## Not for us
- No visible nav (hamburger only), no statement of what the firm does in the first two screens, and a video collage hero. Agency theatre; a biotech buyer wants to know what you do in five seconds.
- Headline set so large that the cookie bar lands inside it.

## Notes for the Tractum design
- Take the two-typeface idea at a tenth of the scale. Nothing else.
