For biotechs

Find out whether your lead candidate has a credible route into the eye, before you commit serious capital.

A good chance of success 71.6%vs 52.0% Ophthalmic drugs move from Phase I to Phase II more often than in any other disease area. We tell you whether yours belongs in the eye before you commit serious capital.
More investable 2.9xvs 1.7x A drug that treats more than one indication returns 2.9x from preclinical to approval, against 1.7x for one alone. The evidence plan and the patent position are what let you claim the second one and show the risk coming down.

For CROs

Three new service lines, built on your own infrastructure. Each one pays back in a different way.

Ophthalmic services 10.2%a year Growth in ophthalmology CRO work, ahead of preclinical services generally. Few laboratories can run an ocular study, so this is work you decline today.
Advanced omics 8.9%a year Growth in CRO and service-provider demand for transcriptome sequencing, the fastest-growing buyer segment. Bulk RNA-seq is the mainstay. We handle the analysis, so you sell the readout without building a bioinformatics team.
Automated analysis 45%of the time back Roughly 45% of working time goes on preparing data rather than analysing it. Agents run the analysis on your existing systems and assemble the report; your scientists keep the sign-off.
